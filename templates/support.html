Help Center

How do I apply for a job?

Go to Find Jobs, choose a job, upload your resume and click Apply.

How can I update my resume?

Open My Applications and click View/Update Resume for the applied job.

How is AI score calculated?

The system compares your resume skills, education and experience with job requirements.

When will I get selection updates?

You can track status in My Applications. Selected candidates may receive email communication.
