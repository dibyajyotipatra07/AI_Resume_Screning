# Welcome to BHEL Haridwar ## Powering India's Progress Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), Haridwar, is one of India's premier engineering and manufacturing enterprises. Established in 1964, the Haridwar unit plays a vital role in the design, manufacture, and supply of power generation equipment for thermal, hydro, nuclear, and renewable energy sectors. With decades of engineering excellence, BHEL Haridwar has contributed significantly to India's industrial growth and energy security. The unit is equipped with advanced manufacturing facilities, research capabilities, and a highly skilled workforce dedicated to delivering world-class products and services. ### Our Vision To be a global engineering enterprise providing sustainable solutions for power generation and industrial development. ### Our Mission * Deliver high-quality engineering products and services. * Promote innovation and technological advancement. * Ensure customer satisfaction through excellence and reliability. * Contribute to national development and environmental sustainability. ### Key Products * Steam Turbines * Hydro Turbines * Gas Turbines * Generators * Industrial Motors * Power Plant Equipment * Renewable Energy Solutions ### Why Choose BHEL Haridwar? ✔ Over 60 years of engineering excellence ✔ Advanced manufacturing infrastructure ✔ Highly skilled technical workforce ✔ Commitment to quality and innovation ✔ Significant contribution to India's power sector ### Building a Stronger Nation Through Engineering Excellence BHEL Haridwar continues to lead the way in providing reliable and efficient power equipment, helping meet the growing energy demands of India and supporting sustainable industrial growth.
