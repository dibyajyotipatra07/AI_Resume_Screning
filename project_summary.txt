# Technical Flow

```text
Resume PDF
      ↓
pdfplumber
      ↓
Resume Text
      ↓
Regex
      ├── Email
      └── Phone

spaCy NER
      ├── Name
      ├── Companies
      ├── Locations
      └── Dates

PhraseMatcher
      ├── Skills
      ├── Technologies
      └── Certifications

Matcher
      ├── Experience
      └── Education

Structured Candidate Data
      ↓
Store in MySQL
      ↓
Compare with Job Description
      ↓
Skill Matching
      ↓
Experience Matching
      ↓
Final Score
      ↓
Candidate Ranking
      ↓
Recruiter Dashboard
```
